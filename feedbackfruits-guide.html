How to Use FeedbackFruits Peer Review

A step-by-step guide for your Research Journal draft exchange
DRAM10026 — Inside Look at Melbourne Theatre Company
This guide walks you through how to submit your Research Journal draft, give feedback to your peers within your IOA group, and read the feedback you receive. The peer exchange uses a tool called FeedbackFruits, which is embedded directly in Canvas. You do not need to create a separate account — just open the activity from your Canvas module page.
1

Read the Instructions

When you open the FeedbackFruits activity, the first thing you will see is the instructions set by your teaching team. Read these carefully — they explain what you are submitting and what to focus on when giving feedback. You will also see which IOA group you belong to (The Circle, The Dock, The Wings, The Stalls, or The Green Room).

The instructions screen shows your group and the task brief.
2

Submit Your Draft

Click Select File or drag your draft into the upload box. You can submit a PDF, Word document, or other supported file types. You can also paste a link (for example, a YouTube video if your entry uses a multimodal format).

Drag your file into the box or click Select File.

Once uploaded, you will see a confirmation. If you need to replace your file, click the trash icon and upload again.

Your submission is confirmed. You can replace it up until the deadline or until a peer begins reviewing it.
Important
You must submit your draft before you can be assigned a peer to review. If you do not submit, you will not be allocated anyone's work, and your peers may not receive feedback either.
3

Give Feedback to Your Peer(s)

Once your peers have also submitted, you will be assigned their work to review. Click Start Reviewing to open their submission alongside the feedback criteria.

When a peer's work is available, you will see it listed here with a Start Reviewing button.

There are several ways to leave feedback on your peer's work:

Annotate the document: Select a section of the document, then choose a criterion. Your comment will be tagged with the page number, and a green speech icon will appear on the document showing where you commented.

Write a general comment: Click Write Comment to provide feedback related to a criterion without annotating any specific part of the document.

Leave ratings: Select ratings for each criterion. You can deselect a rating to leave it blank.

Use the + button: Click the + button to write a comment, select a criterion, and optionally add an annotation.

Video submissions: If your peer has submitted a video, click the purple + icon on the timeline to attach your comment to a specific timestamp.

Make sure you click Post Comment after writing each comment — otherwise your comment will be saved as a draft and may not appear.

Write your comment and click Post Comment to submit it.
If no peer appears yet
If you see a message saying "There is no one to review at this moment", it means your peers have not yet submitted their drafts. Check back later — once they submit, you will be allocated their work.
This message appears when your allocated peer has not yet submitted. Check back later.

When you have completed all your required reviews, your progress will show as complete.

All reviews completed — you can now proceed to read feedback you have received.
4

Read Feedback You Receive

Once your peers (and potentially your tutor) have reviewed your draft, their feedback will appear here. Click View Feedback to read comments and ratings.

If your peers have not yet reviewed your work, you will see this waiting screen.
Once feedback arrives, you will see who reviewed your work and how many comments they left.
What to do with this feedback
Use the peer feedback to inform your revision of this journal entry for your final Research Journal submission. You do not need to agree with every comment — but you should consider each one seriously. If something surprises you or challenges your thinking, that is often where the most productive revision happens.

Quick Reference

  1. Open the FeedbackFruits activity from your Canvas module page.
  2. Read the instructions and check your IOA group.
  3. Upload your draft (PDF, Word, or link).
  4. Once your peers have submitted, review their work using the criteria provided.
  5. Click Post Comment after each comment — do not forget this step.
  6. Check back to read the feedback your peers have left on your own draft.
  7. Use the feedback to revise your entry for the final Research Journal.
Questions? Contact your tutor or the teaching team.
